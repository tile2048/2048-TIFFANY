How to play: Use your arrow keys to move the tiles. When two tiles with the same symbol touch, they get precious!
Hit 'Z' to take back one or more moves.

The display case holds eleven pieces, priced from 70 € to 149,000 €. A heart tag pendant opens at 2, followed by a heart pendant, a pearl bracelet, the Paloma Picasso Love bracelet, an amethyst cocktail ring, a Sommerset ring in rose gold, long Hearths earrings, rope hoop earrings, a Mesh scarf necklace, a narrow Schlunberger bracelet, and the diamond necklace at the top. Slide the 4×4 case with the arrow keys or a swipe; two identical pieces become the next one and add their price to your score. Z takes back a move when the case is crowded. The diamond necklace is the piece to reach.
