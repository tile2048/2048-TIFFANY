# 2048 Tiffany

A fan-made jewellery-case edition of the 2048 sliding-tile puzzle. Eleven pieces with their prices, from a heart tag pendant at 2 to a diamond necklace at 2048.

Una edición de joyería: once piezas con su precio, del colgante con placa de corazón en el 2 al collar de diamantes en el 2048.

**Play:** [English](https://tile2048.github.io/2048-TIFFANY/) · [Español](https://tile2048.github.io/2048-TIFFANY/es/)

## How to play

Arrow keys move the whole board; a swipe does the same on a phone. Two tiles of the same value merge into the next one on the ladder, and the value of the new tile is added to your score. The run ends when the board is full and no two neighbours match, so leave a corner free and plan one merge ahead.
